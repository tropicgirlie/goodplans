# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People in Dublin who want to spend meaningful time with friends, partners, family, or their wider community without carrying all of the planning work themselves. Organisers use the product to turn an idea into a real outing, gathering, or recurring series, while guests need clear details and a simple response flow.

## Product Purpose

Good Plans helps someone find a worthwhile activity, choose the right people and time, send invitations, and manage the event through completion. Success means users discover plans that fit the people they are with, including women-centred gatherings and broad partner or group activities, then create a practical plan without losing the warmth of the original idea.

## Brand Personality

Warm, thoughtful, and playful. The product should feel personal and encouraging while remaining dependable when users are organising dates, invitations, capacity, and event communications.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic corporate events directory, a nightlife-only feed, or a stereotyped “for women” experience limited to pink styling, beauty, or dinner. Avoid treating a partner as a demographic label or assuming what women or men enjoy. Recommendations should reflect the outing context and stated interests.

## Design Principles

1. Start with who the plan is for and what kind of time they want together.
2. Offer varied, specific ideas instead of repeating familiar dinner suggestions.
3. Explain why a recommendation fits and keep organiser decisions reversible.
4. Preserve the original scrapbook character while making planning controls predictable.
5. Keep real-world details, availability, consent, and delivery status honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and clear loading, empty, error, and unavailable states. Use inclusive relationship language. Do not infer gender, sexuality, family structure, personality, or accessibility needs from a name or from who accompanies the user.
