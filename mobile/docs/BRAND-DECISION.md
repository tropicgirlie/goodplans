# Name, mark and domain decision — 4 November 2026

Recommendation: keep the warm illustrated identity, but treat **Good Plans** as a working name until the naming collision is considered. Do not buy a domain yet.

## Name

Good Plans communicates the product well: making time for people and discovering something to do. It is broad enough for friends, partners and parents. However, an existing iPhone/iPad planner already uses **GoodPlans**, with an App Store link on its [official website](https://getgoodplans.com/). The products differ, but the shared category and near-identical name make search, word of mouth and app-store discovery harder. This is a practical naming concern, not trademark clearance.

The preferred direction is a more distinctive name while preserving the current artwork and tone. Keep names easy to say and spell in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, without implying that male partners or family outings are excluded. Any shortlist needs actual app-store, web, domain and relevant trademark checks before adoption. No alternative has been cleared or selected.

## Logo / app icon

The calendar, route and sun mark fits the product's cheerful planning identity. Keep it as a supporting brand illustration in this preview. The detailed collage needs a simpler companion symbol for a small app icon: one recognisable calendar/route motif, strong silhouette and readable colour contrast at small sizes. The preview deliberately has no final AppIcon; a final name should drive the lockup before producing launch assets.

This prototype reuses the existing cream paper, pink accents, illustration and serif headings, with native iOS navigation, forms, calendar review and sharing. It does not replace the website's identity.

## Domain

A dedicated domain is sensible once the name is settled. The existing `goodplans.luana.systems` address can support prototype testing meanwhile. `goodplans.app` was already registered when checked through the [.app registry's RDAP endpoint](https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/goodplans.app). No claim is made about the availability or price of other domains.

After selecting a name: check the exact domain and renewal cost, register it in the creator's own account, configure HTTPS/DNS, verified sending-domain records and redirects, then implement iOS Universal Links against that final domain. No domain has been purchased, and no production branding, DNS or sender settings changed in this task.
